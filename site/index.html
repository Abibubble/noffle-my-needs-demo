About Noffle My Needs

Begin your workday with a smile as you log in to 'Noffle My Needs'. Here you will find a tool to create the conditions you require in order for your workspace to be as accessible and inclusive as possible. Each state of need is a 'Noffle'. Now keep a straight face as you select your Noffle Needs...

About this demo

Noffle My Needs was built by the team A11y Allies for the Trust In SODA and Code Institute Hackathon, in September 2021. The theme was "Building An Accessible Workplace".

A "Noffle" is a need you set on your work status, like "Please speak up". You can set your own Noffles, and see the public Noffles of the people you work with.

How to try it

  • Choose a demo person to view the site as them.
  • Choose Alex Morgan to try the admin pages.
  • There are no real accounts or passwords. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Anything you change is saved only in this browser. You can reset it on the Choose a Demo Person page.
